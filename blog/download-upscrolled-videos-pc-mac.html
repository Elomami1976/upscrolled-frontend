How to Download UpScrolled Videos on PC and Mac

Save UpScrolled videos to Windows or macOS in your browser. No app, no extension, no watermark.

UpScrolled is mainly a phone app, but plenty of people want its videos on a computer: to edit them, keep a backup, or watch them on a bigger screen. The good news is you don't need to install anything. This guide shows how to download UpScrolled videos on a Windows PC or Mac in under a minute, using only your browser.

What You Need

  • A computer running Windows 10/11, macOS, Linux or ChromeOS
  • Any modern browser: Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari, Brave or Opera
  • The link to a public UpScrolled post that contains a video

No account, extension or software is required. The video is processed on our server and sent straight to your browser as a normal MP4 file.

Step 1: Get the UpScrolled Video Link

There are two easy ways to get the link, depending on where you found the video.

From the UpScrolled website on your computer

  1. Open the post in your browser.
  2. Click the address bar and copy the URL (Ctrl+C on Windows, Cmd+C on Mac).

The link will look like https://upscrolled.com/@username/….

From the UpScrolled app on your phone

  1. Open the video and tap Share.
  2. Tap Copy link.
  3. Send the link to your computer, for example by emailing it to yourself, using WhatsApp Web or Telegram Desktop, or through a shared notes app.

Both upscrolled.com/@username/… and older share.upscrolled.com/…/post/… links work with our downloader.

Step 2: Download the Video on Your Computer

  1. Go to upscrolleddownload.com.
  2. Click the input box and paste the link (Ctrl+V or Cmd+V), or click the Paste button inside the box.
  3. Click Download Video and wait a few seconds while the video is prepared.
  4. When you see Your video is ready!, click Download MP4 to save the video, or Download MP3 to save only the audio.

The file is saved in the original quality uploaded by the creator, without any watermark added by us.

Step 3: Find the Downloaded File

Videos are saved with a name like upscrolled-video-1731400000000.mp4 in your browser's download folder:

  • Windows: This PC → Downloads, or press Ctrl+J in Chrome or Edge to open the downloads list.
  • Mac: the Downloads folder in Finder, or click the downloads arrow in Safari's toolbar.

You can rename the file to something easier to remember. MP4 plays in every common player: the Windows Media Player and Photos apps, QuickTime on Mac, and VLC on both.

Tips for Windows Users

  • Choose where files go: in Chrome or Edge, open Settings → Downloads and turn on Ask where to save each file if you want to pick a folder every time.
  • Edit the clip: the free Clipchamp app built into Windows 11 opens MP4 files directly, so you can trim or add captions.
  • Video won't play? Install the free VLC player, which plays almost any format.

Tips for Mac Users

  • Safari download prompt: if Safari asks whether to allow downloads from upscrolleddownload.com, click Allow.
  • Edit in iMovie or QuickTime: both open MP4 files directly. In QuickTime, use Edit → Trim for quick cuts.
  • Send to your iPhone: use AirDrop from Finder to move the video to your phone's Photos app. You can also download straight on the phone; see our iPhone guide.

If the Download Doesn't Work

  • "Invalid link": make sure you copied the full post link, not your profile or the home page. It should contain /@username/ followed by a long ID.
  • Post has no video: UpScrolled posts can be text or photos only. The downloader works for posts that contain a video.
  • Private or deleted post: only public posts can be downloaded.
  • Nothing happens after clicking: check whether your browser blocked the download, try a different browser, or turn off download-blocking extensions for this site.

More fixes are in our troubleshooting guide.

Please Respect Creators

Download videos for personal use, such as watching offline or keeping a backup of your own posts. If you want to re-upload or use someone else's video, ask the creator first and give credit.
