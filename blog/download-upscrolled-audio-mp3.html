Why Save an UpScrolled Video as MP3?

  • Smaller files: audio takes far less storage than video, which is useful on phones that are nearly full.
  • Listen with the screen off: play talks, lectures or music in any audio player, in the car or while you work.
  • Add to playlists: MP3 works in every music app and media player.
  • Reuse your own content: creators can pull the audio from their own posts for podcasts or other platforms.

How to Convert an UpScrolled Video to MP3

  1. Copy the link: in UpScrolled, open the video, tap Share, then Copy link. On a computer, copy the post URL from the address bar.
  2. Paste it: go to upscrolleddownload.com and paste the link into the box, or tap the Paste button.
  3. Prepare it: tap Download Video and wait a few seconds.
  4. Save the audio: when you see Your video is ready!, tap Download MP3.

The audio is extracted on our server and saved as a file named like upscrolled-audio-1731400000000.mp3. You can also tap Download MP4 on the same screen if you want the full video too.

Open the Downloader

On iPhone or iPad

  1. Use Safari and follow the steps above.
  2. When Safari asks, tap Download.
  3. Open the Files app → Downloads to find and play the MP3.

Audio files go to Files, not to the Photos app. To play the file in a music app, open it in Files, tap Share and choose the app. More iPhone tips are in our iPhone guide.

On Android

  1. Use Chrome (or any browser) and follow the steps above.
  2. The MP3 is saved to your Downloads folder.
  3. Open it from the browser's download notification, the Files app, or any music player that scans your storage.

See the Android guide for more help.

On PC or Mac

The steps are the same in any desktop browser. The MP3 lands in your Downloads folder and plays in Windows Media Player, Apple Music, VLC or any other player. Full desktop instructions are in How to Download UpScrolled Videos on PC and Mac.

What Audio Quality Do You Get?

We encode MP3 using high-quality variable bitrate (around 190 kbps on average), which sounds the same as the original for speech and is very close for music on phones and headphones. The audio can never be better than what the creator uploaded, so a clip recorded on a noisy phone mic will still sound like one.

If you need the untouched original audio track (for example for professional editing), download the MP4 instead and extract the audio in your editing software.

Ways to Use Your MP3

  • Listen offline to talks, lessons, recitations or news clips
  • Create a playlist of your favorite sounds from UpScrolled
  • Turn your own UpScrolled videos into podcast episodes
  • Transcribe a clip with a speech-to-text tool

Please only download audio for personal use, and get the creator's permission before reusing their voice or music anywhere public. Songs in videos may also be protected by the music owner's copyright.

Troubleshooting

  • The Download MP3 button keeps loading: long videos take longer to convert. Wait a little, and if it fails, try again or use a shorter clip.
  • "Invalid link": copy the link to the post itself (it contains /@username/ and a long ID), not a profile link.
  • Silent file: some videos have no audio track. Check that the video has sound in the app.
