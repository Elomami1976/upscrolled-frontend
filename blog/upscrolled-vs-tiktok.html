Feed, features, privacy, moderation, creators and downloads, compared side by side.

If you're wondering whether it is worth switching, adding a second app, or staying put, this guide compares the two platforms on the things that actually matter day to day: how the feed decides what you see, what you can post, how each handles moderation and privacy, and what each offers creators.

Quick Verdict

  • Choose TikTok if you want the biggest audience, the most polished video editor, trends and sounds, and creator income you can start earning today.
  • Choose UpScrolled if you want a feed you control (chronological, from accounts you follow), a mix of text, photo and video posts, and a platform that says it does not shadowban or rank posts for engagement.
  • Use both if you're a creator: TikTok for reach, UpScrolled for a closer community that sees your posts in order.

Where Each App Comes From

UpScrolled

UpScrolled was founded by Issam Hijazi, a Palestinian-Jordanian-Australian developer who previously worked in big tech. The app launched in 2025 and stayed small for months. According to the company, Hijazi started building it after feeling that meaningful posts were disappearing from mainstream feeds while misinformation spread.

That changed in late January 2026. Right after TikTok's US business was handed to a new joint venture, many users said they were worried about how feeds and moderation might change. UpScrolled's downloads jumped: it reached the top of Apple's US free-app chart, and the company said it went from roughly 150,000 users to more than 1 million in a matter of days, then to over 2.5 million by early February. A TikTok service outage that same week added to the surge. The fast growth came with growing pains: the company publicly admitted its moderation had not kept up with the flood of new users.

TikTok

TikTok is owned by China's ByteDance and has been the dominant short-video app worldwide since the late 2010s, with well over a billion monthly users. In the United States, after years of legal and political pressure, the app's US operations were moved in January 2026 into TikTok USDS Joint Venture, majority-owned by investors including Oracle, Silver Lake and MGX, with ByteDance keeping a minority stake. For users outside the US, the app works much as it always has.

The Feed and the Algorithm

This is the single biggest difference between the two apps.

TikTok is built around the For You feed. Its recommendation system watches how long you linger, rewatch, like, comment and share, and uses that to pick each next video. It is very good at it, which is why TikTok feels addictive, and why new creators can go viral without any followers. The downside is that you have limited control: what you see is what the algorithm thinks will keep you watching.

UpScrolled takes the opposite approach. Its Following feed shows posts from accounts you follow in chronological order, and a separate Discover page surfaces trending topics and new accounts. The company says it does not use engagement-maximizing ranking, describing its approach as time-based with some randomness, with "no manipulation." In practice this feels closer to early Twitter or Instagram than to TikTok: you see what the people you chose posted, newest first.

Bottom line: TikTok is better for discovery and endless entertainment; UpScrolled is better if you want to stay in control of your feed and not miss posts from people you follow.

What You Can Post

TikTok is a video-first app. You get a deep set of creative tools: a huge sound library, filters and effects, green screen, duets and stitches, templates, LIVE streaming and photo carousels. Its sister app CapCut adds even more advanced editing.

UpScrolled is a mixed-media social network. You can post short videos (with a simpler built-in editor), photos and text, and repost other people's posts, with likes, comments and direct messages. Many reviewers have noted its layout feels closer to X (Twitter) than to TikTok, and early users posted a lot of text and photos, not only video.

If your content depends on trending audio, effects and polished edits, TikTok is the stronger tool. If you want to share thoughts, links, photos and videos in one place, UpScrolled is more flexible.

Moderation and Free Expression

Much of UpScrolled's early growth came from users who felt their posts were being suppressed elsewhere, especially posts about Palestine and other political topics. UpScrolled positions itself as against censorship and shadowbanning, and says it removes content that is illegal (for example drug sales) rather than content that is merely controversial. It has also announced a community-notes-style feature for adding context to posts.

TikTok has detailed Community Guidelines and moderates at enormous scale using automated systems and human reviewers. That makes it more predictable for advertisers and families, but users and creators regularly complain about videos being removed, restricted or quietly pushed down without explanation.

Keep in mind that a lighter-touch policy cuts both ways: UpScrolled may let you say more, but you may also see more content you would rather not. Its moderation team is also much smaller and, by the company's own admission, struggled during the early-2026 rush of new users.

Privacy and Data

UpScrolled says it does not share user data with third parties for marketing or profiling. It is exploring advertising, but says any ads would be run in-house, without third-party ad networks or tracking, and not targeted using personal data. It also promises clean data exports and reliable deletion of your content.

TikTok collects extensive data to power its recommendations and its personalized ad business, as described in its privacy policy. In the US, user data and the recommendation algorithm are now overseen by the USDS joint venture, with Oracle as a security partner.

Note that UpScrolled's privacy commitments are company statements and have not been independently audited. As with any app, read the current privacy policy before signing up.

Creators: Reach and Money

Reach. TikTok wins on raw numbers. Its algorithm can put a brand-new account in front of millions of people overnight. On UpScrolled, the chronological feed means you mostly reach the people who follow you, so growth is slower, but your followers actually see your posts.

Money. TikTok has mature monetization: the Creator Rewards Program for longer videos, LIVE gifts, subscriptions, the TikTok Creator Marketplace for brand deals, and affiliate sales through TikTok Shop (eligibility and availability vary by country). UpScrolled has announced creator payouts, branded content and community support tools, but these were still on its roadmap in the latest public reporting, so you should not count on direct earnings there yet.

Strategy for creators: post on TikTok for discovery and income, and cross-post to UpScrolled to build a loyal audience you are less likely to lose to an algorithm change.

Audience Size and Community

There is no contest on size: TikTok has over a billion monthly users across almost every country and interest. UpScrolled reported over 2.5 million registered users in early February 2026, with its top download markets in the United States, United Kingdom, Canada and Australia. Daily activity is much smaller than total sign-ups, as is normal for a new app.

The two communities also feel different. TikTok is broad: comedy, dance, beauty, gaming, education, news and every niche in between. UpScrolled's early community leaned toward news, politics, activism and conversation, with a strong presence of Muslim and Arab users and others who felt under-served by bigger platforms. As the app grows, expect that mix to broaden.

Which One Should You Use?

Pick UpScrolled if you…

  • Want a chronological feed from people you follow, without algorithmic ranking
  • Like mixing text, photo and video posts in one app
  • Care about a platform that says it does not shadowban or sell data for ad targeting
  • Want to be early on a growing community

Pick TikTok if you…

  • Want endless, highly personalized entertainment
  • Make video content that relies on trends, sounds and effects
  • Need the largest possible audience or want to earn from your content now
  • Prefer a mature app with well-defined safety and parental controls

Or use both

The apps are different enough that they complement each other. A common setup is TikTok for discovery and entertainment, and UpScrolled for following specific people and conversations in order.
